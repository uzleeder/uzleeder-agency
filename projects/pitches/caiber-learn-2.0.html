Deterministic scoring engine

06 — Competitive strategy
Why not just YouTube, ChatGPT or a local course?
Alternative
Its limit
CAIBER
YouTube / free content
Unstructured videos, no feedback
Step-by-step diagnostic path with active testing
General AI (ChatGPT)
Direct answers, no curriculum memory, can get rules wrong
Tied to curriculum rubrics, tracks long-term gaps, Socratic guidance
Local learning centers
Fixed schedule, high cost, location-bound
Available anywhere 24/7, plus B2B tools that cut centers' manual work
An AI chat box is not a moat. Ours is the combination: structured curriculum + deterministic gap tracking + B2B teacher workflows.
