How it works

Six steps, every one recorded.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Submit your background, skills, experience, portfolio, interests and intended contribution.

  2. 2

    Review the policy

    Read the Partner Program Policy, the privacy notice and the qualification terms, and acknowledge them before the qualification stage.

  3. 3

    Choose or receive a qualification project

    Path A — UzLeeder-assigned project: a defined brief with deliverables, acceptance criteria, timeline, resources, compensation and IP terms. Path B — independent project: you propose and build a project that shows what you can do.

  4. 4

    Demonstrate your abilities

    Quality, problem-solving, maintainability, security, product thinking, communication and reliability — the criteria are shared before work begins.

  5. 5

    Receive a decision

    Approval, a request for more information, another qualification opportunity, or a decline. Qualification alone is not a legal partnership.

  6. 6

    Formalize the partnership

    Selected applicants receive a written agreement and onboarding steps. A partnership is effective only under that agreement.

The process can vary by role and project. There is no automatic approval and no fixed acceptance timeline. Full rules: Partner Program Policy, version 1.0.
