Your application

    Before you start

    • Have links to your work ready — a portfolio, GitHub, Behance or a live project.
    • Describe what you did, not only the team.
    • Don't include passwords, ID numbers, bank details or a home address.
    • Your progress stays in this browser tab until you submit.

    What happens next

    1. Review — we read your application and evidence.
    2. Questions — we may ask for more information.
    3. Qualification — possibly a short, explained task.
    4. Trial — only if selected, with terms shown first.
    5. Decision — recorded, with a reason.

    No fixed timeline. Updates appear on your private application page.
