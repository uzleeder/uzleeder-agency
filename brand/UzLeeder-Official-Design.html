02 · Logo

Primary logo

Lockup

The Λ mark + the wordmark UzLeeder + the slogan FOR LEADERS. This is the primary logo for the website header, documents and presentations.

The Λ mark

A peak — leadership and direction — drawn as two strokes: the red outer Λ and the inner chevron. Used alone for the favicon, app icon, cursor, avatars and small spaces.

03 · Logo

Construction & clear space

Geometry (48 × 44 grid)

  • Outer Λ: points (4,41) → (24,5) → (44,41), stroke 7, miter joins.
  • Inner chevron: (14,41) → (24,23) → (34,41), stroke 5.5.
  • Both strokes share the same base line; the inner peak sits exactly half-way.
  • Colours: outer Brand Red; inner Ink Navy on light, White on dark.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the inner chevron (≈ 40% of the mark height). No text, edges or images inside it.

Minimum size

Λ mark: 16 px / 5 mm. Full lockup: 120 px / 32 mm wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

The dashed line marks the minimum clear space.

06 · Emblem

Official emblem

What it is

The round seal of UzLeeder: ink-navy disc, red rim and dotted ring, the Λ peak with a white summit, “UZLEEDER” on top, “·FOR·LEADERS·” below and “EST. 2026”.

Where it is used

  • The UzLeeder brand section of the website (the ring rotates slowly; the centre stays still).
  • The footer (stamp style, follows the theme).
  • Official moments: certificates, awards, social cover images.

Rules

  • Not a replacement for the logo in the header.
  • Never crop the ring text or change “EST. 2026”.
  • Minimum size 18 mm / 64 px so the lettering stays readable.

07 · Stamp

Document stamp

The stamp edition

The emblem without the disc — one ink colour (Red · light theme) with the inner Λ in Ink Navy, on a transparent background, so it prints like a real rubber stamp.

How to place it

  • Bottom right of a document, next to the signature line.
  • Rotated −8°, blend mode “multiply”, 30–36 mm wide.
  • Only on documents issued by UzLeeder: certificates, service & pricing documents, contracts.

Honesty rule

The stamp marks a document as issued by UzLeeder itself. It is not third-party certification and must never be presented as one.

01 ——— SERVICES

Three things UzLeeder builds.

Buttons

Primary = red fill, white text, one per view. Secondary = outline. Radius 10 px.

Section kicker

Two-digit red number + thin line + uppercase label. Numbers follow the order on the page.

Status pills

Only honest statuses: Live client project · Product available for deployment · Demonstration · In development · Private project · Concept · Archived.

Cards

Surface colour, 1 px border, radius 14–18 px, no heavy shadows; lift 3 px on hover.

The Λ mark, tilted −28° like a system arrow (22 × 24 px). Colours swap on links.

Themes

Dark and light versions of every screen; the visitor’s choice is remembered.

12 · Motion

Motion & intro

<UzLeeder>

<FOR LEADERS>

AGI // SYSTEM INITIALIZATION → … → READY

SALAAM

NEW ERA

The intro — locked

  1. “<”, then U·z·L·e·e·d·e·r letter by letter, then “>”.
  2. “<For leaders>” types in below.
  3. The brackets fold into the Λ mark.
  4. System initialization line (V5): “AGI //” + SYSTEM INITIALIZATION · LOADING UZLEEDER · CONNECTING SYSTEMS · HUMAN CONTROL · ON · READY, one segment of a five-part rail per state (no percentages).
  5. SALAAM / NEW ERA — then the homepage.

The core animation must not be redesigned. Only speed, smoothness and accessibility may improve.

Everywhere else

  • Short, calm transitions (200–600 ms, ease-out).
  • Motion explains state; it is never decoration.
  • “Reduce motion”: the intro shows the still logo and READY, then opens.

14 · Signature

Digital signature — Crafted by UzLeeder

The signature at the very bottom of every website UzLeeder builds. It replaces the old crown badge and uses the Λ mark.

Client Name© 2026 Client Name
CRAFTED BYUzLeeder
Client Name© 2026 Client Name
CRAFTED BYUzLeeder

Dark sites · light sites (sample client footers)

Anatomy

  • CRAFTED BY — monospace, 11 px, uppercase, tracking .28em, muted colour.
  • Λ mark — 22 × 20 px, the standard two strokes (7 / 5.5).
  • Wordmark — Outfit 800, 17 px: Uz in ink (white on dark) + Leeder in red.
  • Gap 12 px, centred, 14 px above and below. Small size: 9.5 / 18 × 16 / 14 px.
  • Motion — at rest only the Λ sways around its base (2.8 s loop); “CRAFTED BY” stays still. On hover or focus the wordmark types, like the intro: <UzLeeder> → <LeeCodes> → <Max's Code>; leaving returns it to the wordmark. Reduce motion: no waving, no typing.

How to add it

One line in the site's footer (recommended):

<script src="https://uzleeder.vercel.app/crafted.js" defer></script>
<uzleeder-crafted theme="dark"></uzleeder-crafted>

theme dark · light · auto — align center · start · end — size md · sm — fonts="none" skips the Outfit subset. It renders in its own Shadow DOM, so the site's CSS cannot change it. A script-free HTML version is on /brand/crafted-by.html.

Rules

  • Only on websites UzLeeder really built — for client sites, agreed in the contract or with the client.
  • Small, at the very bottom of the footer: it signs the work, it does not advertise.
  • Links to uzleeder.vercel.app; no cookies, no tracking.
  • No crown, no recolouring, no other logo in its place.

Not the same as the project signature

The Workspace project signature (UZLEEDER · PROJECT COMPLETED / READY FOR DEPLOYMENT / DEMONSTRATION · PROJECT ID · SALAAM NEW ERA.) is an internal delivery mark on a project record. “Crafted by UzLeeder” is the public signature on a live website. Neither is a certification.

15 · Voice

Voice & copy

Tone

Confident · technical · clear · human · calm · ambitious. A builder who understands business — not a marketing template.

Prefer

  • “Real products. Real engineering.”
  • “AI-assisted development.”
  • “Designed, directed, validated, shipped.”
  • Concrete facts: what it is, who it is for, what it does.

Avoid

  • “Revolutionary”, “world-class”, “industry-leading”, “passionate developer”.
  • Invented clients, numbers, reviews or guarantees.
  • Promising perfect AI or unlimited free work.

AI team — how to talk about it

  • Max — Founder & CEO, the human decision-maker.
  • Claude — Chief Architect (an AI workflow role).
  • Codex — AI Coding Engineer; other tools only in roles they really play.
  • Always add: AI systems are part of Max’s AI-assisted workflow — not employees or owners.

Numbers

Only verified product data (for example 5,653 exercises, 31 topics, 1,100+ airlines) — labelled as product data, never as revenue or results.

Prices

Always “from $…”, with the final price fixed in the agreed scope.
